# About 'An AI-assisted note on the dp-rank of ordered abelian groups'

I made [this note](latex/out/dprank_main.pdf) to practice proving and writing mathematical contents using LLMs.
To do so, I chose a calculation of the dp-rank of ordered abelian groups as a topic, which is a well-known result independently shown by Farré, and by Halevi and Hasson.
I'm recently working in model theory of ordered abelian groups and though I hadn't read their papers in detail, I had proof ideas which I was confident about.
So this topic was suitable for my AI training.
(However, most of those ideas turned out to be wrong and I had to reconsider valid ideas.)
This note contains no new result.
But I believe that the proof is concise and naïve and might help someone's understanging.

I wrote down only sketches of proofs in [this file](latex/dprank_information.tex) and then instructed ChatGPT 5.6 Sol to complete the details.
The generated proofs were correct and contained no serious error.
Although I was basically satisfied with this result, several parts were opaque, redundant, or disorganized.
I revised them using Grok 4.6 (because ChatGPT usage had run out) first, and then by hand.
Admittedly they are not perfect, LLMs handled complicated mathematical tasks and saved much time.
I anticipate that AI-assisted mathematical writing will be much more common, and that mathematicians will be able to largely focus on creative tasks (which does not include constructing the details of proofs, of course).
